# IVW (Implied Volatility Walls): study and app backtest (2026-09-19)

## What IVW is

OmegaTools' "Implied Volatility Walls" is an invite-only TradingView script from
October 2024. Its code is private, so it was rebuilt from the published
description. It has four parts:

* **Walls**: the day's open ± a percentile of past sessions' largest move away from
  their open ("price tends to move most of the time by the same amount").
* **Cumulative volatility**: the summed high-low range, bucketed into low, medium
  or high regimes.
* **Machine-learning volatility forecast** ("Exp. Vol"). It is rebuilt here as a
  HAR range forecast, the benchmark that the literature finds ML rarely beats
  (HAR beats GARCH by 35–40%, while LSTMs add a non-significant 4–6%).
* **Liquidation Bubbles** (OmegaTools' open-source indicator): a bar whose extreme
  is ≥ 2σ beyond an inverse-volume-weighted equilibrium, *and* whose volume is
  ≥ 2σ above normal.

A "VIX adjustment" of the walls was tested with CBOE VIX/VXN/GVZ/OVX/EVZ and
Deribit DVOL.

## Research harness

scratchpad `ivw/ivw_study.py`, `ivw_components.py` and `ivw_detail.py`, on MT5 M5
data for 16 markets (2013–2026; indices and oil only from 2024).

* Costs: the entry bar's spread plus slippage.
* One trade a day. Flat at the end of the UTC day.
* The in-sample period is 2013–2019 and the out-of-sample period is 2020–2026.
* Each component is recorded at entry, using only completed bars. The first pass
  read the liquidation bubble from the entry bar itself (look-ahead, which inflated
  breakout+bubble to +0.147R); this was fixed before every number below.

### Findings, pooled over markets (q80 walls)

| Mode | 2013–19 | 2020–26 |
|---|---|---|
| Fade the wall (what the indicator is sold for) | −0.122R (t −8.8) | −0.056R (t −5.6) |
| Fade after a bubble bar closes back inside | −0.153R | −0.113R |
| Breakout (first M5 close through a wall) | −0.093R | +0.005R |
| Breakout, regime low | −0.085R | +0.044R (t 2.6) |
| Breakout, forecast contracting | −0.015R | +0.061R (t 2.5) |
| Breakout, bubble on the breakout bar | −0.071R | +0.055R (t 2.4) |

Implied-vol walls did not help: breakout −0.044R vs −0.059R raw on the same 9
markets, and fade was worse.

Searching for the best combination covered 894 rules. Each was ranked on
2013–2019 only and then judged on 2020–2026. About 22 rules would clear an
in-sample t of 2 by luck alone.

* **Rule B** (shipped): breakout of the 90th-percentile wall, regime low, bubble.
  2013–19 +0.045R (t 0.9, n 389); 2020–26 **+0.152R (t 3.3, n 512)**, positive on 10 of 10
  markets with ≥ 10 trades.
* **Rule A**: q70, forecast contracting, bubble. 2013–19 +0.096R (t 1.6);
  2020–26 +0.100R (t 2.6, n 931), with a 25% drawdown at 1%.

## Backend: `IVW_v1` (backend/strategies/strategy_ivw)

The engine implements rule B. It takes completed sessions from D1 bars and the
signal from M5 bars.

* A D1 row counts as a session when its tick volume reaches 31% of the median of it
  and the 20 rows before it. This matches the study's "≥ 100 M5 bars" rule on
  703/704 FX days and on every index, metal and oil day.
* The stop is half a wall-width, the target 1:2, and the position is flat at the
  end of the UTC day (`on_position_bar`, live via `LIVE_POSITION_EXITS`).
* `tests/test_ivw_strategy.py` feeds the engine through `BarFeed` and requires the
  study's exact signal bars, directions and stops.
* The measured exits are in `STRATEGY_DEFAULTS["IVW_v1"]`, including `min_rr` 2.0.
  The new `min_rr_by_strategy` lets that lower the account's min R:R for IVW
  signals only; the shipped 3.0 rejected every 1:2 signal.

## Caveats

* The combination was chosen from 894 candidates. Its in-sample evidence is weak
  (rule B: +0.045R, t 0.9, n 389 on 2013–19), and the case rests on 2020–26 alone.
* The components flipped sign between periods, so a regime change could flip them
  back. The losing years were 2020 (−11R) and 2025 (−7R).
* Trades on correlated markets (EURUSD/GBPUSD, BTCUSD/ETHUSD) are not independent.
