How we manage risk

Every decision comes from tested rules, executed by software, and bounded by hard limits that are enforced by the trading system itself.

  • Rules, not moods

    Every entry and exit comes from a strategy that was researched and tested before it traded real money. Nothing is decided on impulse on the day.

  • Hard risk limits

    Position size, risk per strategy and daily loss limits are enforced by the trading system itself, not left to judgement on the day.

  • More than one engine

    Several strategies across several markets, so results do not depend on a single idea working.

  • Open to inspection

    Closed trades are published to your dashboard with their results, and every movement of your money is on your statement.

Questions

How is my balance worked out?

Your balance moves by the same percentage as the programme's trading results from the day your money arrives. If the programme gains 3% after you join, your balance gains 3%. Money added later only shares in results from the day it arrives, so nobody benefits from profit made before their money was invested.

Can I really see the trades?

Yes. Your dashboard lists the trades we close, with the market, direction, date and result. We do not publish the strategy settings behind them, because publishing them would stop them working.

How do I get my money out?

Request a withdrawal in your dashboard. It is paid to the bank account we hold for you. Requests within the standard limit are paid within the notice period; larger or earlier ones are reviewed with you first.

What if the programme loses money?

Your balance falls by the same percentage. No management fee is charged for a period without profit, and the performance fee is only charged on profit above your own previous high point, so after a loss nothing is charged until that loss has been earned back. You can lose money investing; only invest what you can leave invested through a bad period.

When are fees taken?

Every two months, at the end of February, April, June, August, October and December. Each fee appears on your statement and in your dashboard.

Is there an app?

Yes. On Android, install it from the download link below. On iPhone, open the investor dashboard in Safari and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Can I close my account?

Yes, from the dashboard. We pay your balance to your bank account on file, send a final statement, and delete your personal details, keeping only the records the law requires.
