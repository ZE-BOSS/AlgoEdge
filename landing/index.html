Approach

Systematic, multi-strategy and volatility-aware. Decisions are made by tested rules, executed by software, and bounded by hard risk limits.

  • Rules, not moods

    Every entry and exit comes from a strategy that was researched and tested before it traded real money. Nothing is discretionary on the day.

  • Risk first

    Position sizing, per-strategy risk caps and daily loss limits are enforced in the trading system itself, not left to judgement.

  • Several engines

    More than one strategy, across more than one market, so the programme does not depend on a single idea working.

  • Kept private

    We describe the approach, not the parameters. Publishing the rules would be the fastest way to stop them working.

Questions

What exactly do I own?

Units in the pool. Everyone's units are priced the same, so when the pool gains or loses, every holding moves by the same percentage. Someone who joins later buys at the later price, so they never share in profit made before their money arrived — and you never share in theirs.

How do I get my money out?

Request a withdrawal in the app. It is paid to the bank account we hold for you — never to an account typed into the request. Requests within the standard limit are paid within the notice period; larger or earlier ones are reviewed with you first.

What if the fund loses money?

Your holding falls with the unit price. The performance fee is charged only on profit above your own previous high point, so after a loss nothing is charged until that loss has been earned back. You can lose money investing; only invest what you can leave invested through a bad period.

How are fees taken?

Monthly, by cancelling a small number of your units at the month-end price. Each fee is a line on your statement, split into management and performance.

Is there an app?

Yes. On Android, install it from the download link below. On iPhone, open the investor site in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.

Can I close my account?

Yes, from the app. We sell your units, pay the proceeds to your account on file, send a final statement, and delete your personal details.
