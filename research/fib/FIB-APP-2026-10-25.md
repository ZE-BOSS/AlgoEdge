# FibTrend_v1 in the app: backtest results and parity, 2026-10-25

`FibTrend_v1` (backend/strategies/strategy_fib) is research variant G/X from
[FIB-USES-2026-10-25.md](FIB-USES-2026-10-25.md), wired into the backtester, the portfolio backtester, live
trading and the UI.

## How it trades

**Structure (H4):**
- H4 swings are confirmed with 5 bars on each side, or 3 bars on the real CFDs.
- A leg counts if it is at least 3 ATR long.
- The leg must run with the 50-day trend: the close on the right side of SMA(300 H4) and that SMA sloping the same way.

**Execution (M15):**
- **Synthetics (`fib` mode):** enter on the first M15 bar that trades back to Fib 0.5. If the pullback reached 0.5 before the leg was confirmed, it is a missed entry and no trade.
- **8 real CFDs (`immediate` mode, set by default through strategy_defaults):** enter at the first M15 open after the leg is confirmed.
- The setup is void if price runs beyond the leg's end first, and it expires after 30 H4 bars.

**Exit:**
- Initial stop at Fib 0.786 (Fib 0.5 in immediate mode).
- No target: a 3×H4-ATR chandelier trail behind the best H4 close since entry, which only ever tightens.

## Backtest = live
- **Signals:** `tests/test_fib_trend.py` runs the live feeder (capped fetches, a scan clock with a 7.5-hour outage) against the backtest feeder. They produce the **same signals, on the same bars, at the same prices**.
- **Trailing stop:** the backtest's position slice and a live fetch give an identical level.
- **Window start:** H4 structure is built from the last N complete H4 bars, so it does not depend on where the M15 window starts.
- **Against the research:** on Step Index 200 the app found every research trade since March 2026, at identical Fib levels.

## Settings
`scripts/run_app_backtest.py` (the Backtester page's code path) with:
- costs, swap and slippage from MT5;
- $10,000 starting balance, 1% of balance per trade, compounded;
- one position per market, no pyramiding;
- `ALGOEDGE_MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET=0`, which is correct for Deriv (see below).

The combined books are one $10k account trading every market in the group.

## Read before trading it
- **History limits:**
  - US indices on this terminal start in January 2024; Step 200/500 in April 2024; Step 300/400 in November 2024.
  - Synthetic M15 history reaches back only to December 2023.
  - The strategy then needs about 80 days of warm-up, so "3 years" is really 2–2.5 years for many markets.
- **Minimum lot:**
  - At $10k and 1% risk, Step 300/400/500 refuse most signals. The 0.1-lot minimum risks $100–235 on these stops, so these markets need roughly $15–20k.
  - The app correctly skips those trades, which is why they show 2–13 trades.
- **Not proven:**
  - All 20 markets together: t +1.75.
  - 9 variants were tested to find this one.
  - Wall Street 30, Step Index and GBPJPY lose.
  - The edge is concentrated in XAUUSD, XAGUSD, US Tech 100, Volatility 10/25 and Multi Step 2.
  - Forward-test on demo before real money.
- **Drawdown:** the 20-market book reached 27.6% at 1% risk. At 0.5% it is about half that.
- **`.env` offset:** this machine pins `ALGOEDGE_MT5_SERVER_UTC_OFFSET=+3`, but the data fetcher measures Deriv at +0h.
  - It shifts every bar by 3h, which put this strategy's H4 grid off the broker's and turned Multi Step 2 from +0.49R into −0.04R.
  - Per the app's own warning, it puts every session strategy 3 hours off.
  - Backtests and live share the setting, so they stay consistent with each other, but both are wrong.
