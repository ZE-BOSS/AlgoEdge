# Can Fibonacci be used profitably? Research and backtests, 2026-10-25

This follows [FIB-REPORT-2026-10-25.md](FIB-REPORT-2026-10-25.md).
Code: `fib_backtest.py` (now with the trend filter, trailing exit and market-entry control), `fib_v2.py` ($10k accounts), `fib_surrogate.py` (sign-flip null), `fib_book.py` (combined book), `fib_dcheck.py` (by year).

## Published evidence
- An automatic-identification study on three equity markets: prices bounce at zones, but Fibonacci zones are no different from non-Fibonacci ones.
- An arXiv survey of retracement data found no statistically significant retracement levels.
- An HKUST student backtest on Vanguard ETFs: Fibonacci strategies underperformed buy-and-hold.
- No peer-reviewed study shows an edge for Fibonacci with a trend filter.

## Method
- **Variants tested:**
  - A: the user's rules
  - B: CHoCH + order block
  - C–G: entries aligned with the 50-day trend (SMA 300 H4 and its slope), with target 0, a 1.618 extension, or a 3×ATR chandelier trailing stop, at 0.382 or 0.5 entries, with K=3 or K=5 legs
  - H: trailing stop without the trend filter
  - X: control, trend + trailing stop with an immediate entry and no Fib
- **Null:** 20 sign-flip surrogates of each real market. Every bar is mirrored around its open, which keeps volatility, wicks and spread but destroys direction. This puts the harness's own fill biases into the null.
  - A Gaussian random walk showed those biases are about +0.1R for trailing exits and −0.05R for fixed targets.

## Caveats
- **Costs and fills:**
  - Costs are the spread only.
  - Stops fill exactly at the stop.
  - **Swap is not included.** Trailing trades hold for days to weeks, and Deriv charges swap on synthetics.
- **History:** US indices start in January 2024 and Step 200–500 start in 2024, so "3 years" is shorter for those markets.
- **Multiple testing:** 9 variants × 2 books were tested. With 20 surrogates the smallest p is <0.05, which does not survive a correction for 18 tests. G on synthetics is a forward-test candidate, not a proven edge.
- **Concentration:**
  - X's real-market profit comes mostly from the 2024–2026 rally in gold and silver, plus BTCUSD.
  - Wall Street 30 loses with X (p 0.95).
  - X is essentially TrendBreakout_v1, which is already in the app.
